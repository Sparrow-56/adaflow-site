SMS Opt-In

Last updated: May 22, 2026

AdaFlow sends operational and customer-service text messages only to people who opt in. Messages may include responses to user-submitted inquiries, appointment coordination, follow-up questions, and service updates.

How to opt in

End users opt in by submitting the AdaFlow contact form at https://adaflow.io/#contact. The form includes a clearly visible SMS consent checkbox. The checkbox is unchecked by default and must be selected by the user before AdaFlow sends SMS messages.

Consent checkbox language: "I agree to receive text messages from AdaFlow about my inquiry, appointments, follow-ups, and service updates. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase."

Message Details

  • Message frequency varies based on account activity and service requests.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe from AdaFlow text messages.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact sparrow@adaflow.io.

Consent

By selecting the SMS consent checkbox and submitting the contact form, you consent to receive operational and customer-service SMS messages from AdaFlow. Consent can be revoked at any time by replying STOP.

Privacy

AdaFlow does not sell phone numbers, mobile opt-in data, text message consent, or SMS consent information. AdaFlow does not share mobile opt-in data, text message consent, or SMS consent information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions for more details.
