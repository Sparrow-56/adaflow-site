SMS Opt-In

Last updated: May 21, 2026

AdaFlow sends operational and customer-service text messages for opted-in users. Messages may include service request confirmations, status updates, lead activity alerts, and follow-up reminders tied to an AdaFlow account or service request.

How to opt in

Text the keyword below to AdaFlow's registered SMS number.

START

Message Details

  • Message frequency varies based on account activity and service requests.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe from AdaFlow text messages.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact sparrow@adaflow.io.

Consent

By texting START, you consent to receive operational and customer-service SMS messages from AdaFlow. Consent can be revoked at any time by replying STOP.

Privacy

AdaFlow does not sell phone numbers or share SMS consent for third-party marketing. See the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions for more details.
